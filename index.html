About Us

Spreading Light Through Knowledge

In the Name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate

Imamia Online Quran School is not just an online academy — it is a responsibility, a trust, and a mission.

We provide high-quality online Quran education to students around the world. Our goal is to help children and adults learn the Holy Quran with correct Tajweed, proper pronunciation, and beautiful recitation — all from the comfort and safety of their homes.

Our Vision

Our foundation is based on Shia (Fiqh Jafaria) teachings. We aim to spread the light of the Quran with the guidance of Ahlulbayt (A.S.) to homes worldwide. At the same time, we respectfully provide education to Sunni (Fiqh Hanafi) students as well.

We believe in unity, respect, and authentic Islamic learning.

Our Classes

  • Classes are held Monday to Friday (5 days a week)
  • Flexible scheduling available if fewer days are required
  • One-to-one personalized sessions
  • Qualified male and female teachers
  • Students from all countries are welcome

Classes are conducted live via Microsoft Teams. If Teams is not accessible in your area, we also offer WhatsApp as an alternative.

Online Quran Classes with Qualified Teachers

Imamia Online Quran School offers online Quran classes for children and adults who want to read the Holy Quran correctly. Every class is a live one-to-one session with a qualified male or female teacher, so each student gets personal attention and learns at a comfortable pace.

Learn Quran Online with Tajweed

Beginners start with Basic Noorani Qaida to learn the Arabic letters and their pronunciation. Students then move on to reading the Quran with Tajweed and Tarteel, para by para, with a teacher who corrects every mistake.

Shia and Sunni Fiqh Courses

Along with Quran reading we teach Basic Fiqa Jafariya for Shia students and Basic Fiqa Hanafi for Sunni students, as well as daily Duas and Azkar.

Flexible Online Quran Classes for Students from Any Country

Classes run Monday to Friday at flexible timings, in Urdu, English or Arabic, through Microsoft Teams or WhatsApp. Start with a 3-day free trial and see our teaching style before you decide.

Install Imamia Quran School

Add our app to your home screen. It opens full screen like a normal app, and Qaida & Quran pages work offline after the first download.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Computer (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Or open the browser menu and choose Install Imamia Online Quran School.
